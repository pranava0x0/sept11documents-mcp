    Contact
    www.cdc.gov/wtc · 1-888-982-4748 CDC, about the Program
    Provides
    medical monitoring and treatment for WTC-related conditions CDC, about the Program
    Deadline
    There is no deadline to enroll. CDC, about the Program
    Time window
    between September 11, 2001 and July 31, 2002 CDC, supporting documentation Survivors: lived, worked, or attended school, daycare or adult daycare in the NYC Disaster Area
    Zone
    NYC Disaster Area, defined on the CDC page ↗ The boundary is defined on the CDC eligible-groups page, which is not captured in this checkout, so it is linked rather than quoted.
    Separate program
    Is a separate 9/11 assistance program from the September 11th Victim Compensation Fund (VCF). CDC, about the Program

    Rules the program states

    • Supporting documentation must show your full name and the time period for your covered activity CDC, supporting documentation
    • Incomplete or insufficient supporting documentation is the top reason for application processing delays. CDC, supporting documentation

    Records that prove presence

    • Employment
      • Letter from your employer or union stating your activities, location, days, and hours performing 9/11-related activities CDC, supporting documentation responders
      • A pay stub if it shows your work location between September 11, 2001 and July 31, 2002 CDC, supporting documentation survivors
      • Police memo book(including a copy of the cover page) that shows the days, times, and hours worked at a 9/11-related site. CDC, supporting documentation responders
    • Residence
      • A lease or utility bill with your name showing your residence between September 11, 2001 and July 31, 2002 CDC, supporting documentation survivors
    • School
      • A School Transcript from the Fall 2001 or Spring 2002 semester (or both) CDC, supporting documentation survivors
    • If no official document can be found
      • you can submit a letter written by someone who can confirm your eligibility details CDC, supporting documentation (the CDC page calls this a third-party attestation)

    Official pages: Supporting documentation · Eligible groups and the NYC Disaster Area · About the Program

    U.S. Department of Justice

    September 11th Victim Compensation Fund

    Contact
    www.vcf.gov · 1-855-885-1555 VCF Policies and Procedures
    Deadline
    submit your complete claim no later than October 1, 2090 VCF Policies and Procedures Registration has its own deadline applicable to your individual circumstances VCF Policies and Procedures
    Time window
    between September 11, 2001, and May 30, 2002 VCF Policies and Procedures The World Trade Center site or the NYC Exposure Zone
    Zone
    VCF NYC Exposure Zone: runs south of Canal Street, river to river, and from the Hudson River to the intersection of Canal Street and East Broadway VCF Policies and Procedures north on East Broadway to Clinton Street, and east on Clinton Street to the East River VCF Policies and Procedures The routes of debris removal apply only to those individuals who loaded, unloaded, or drove the trucks containing WTC debris VCF Policies and Procedures Map ↗
    Separate program
    Enrollment in the WTC Health Program does not automatically register you with the VCF VCF Policies and Procedures

    Rules the program states

    • Registration is not the same as filing a claim. VCF Policies and Procedures
    • There are no requirements for the minimum amount of time an individual needed to be at a site VCF Policies and Procedures

    Records that prove presence

    • Third-party verification (primary)
      • Sent directly to the VCF from the employer or other third party, with specific dates and locations, is often sufficient to verify presence VCF Policies and Procedures
      • A claimant with a listed employer, entity or union must demonstrate that he/she sought to obtain presence information from the listed VCF Policies and Procedures (before the VCF relies on witness statements)
    • Contemporaneous documents (primary)
      • At least two documents, whether from the list below or otherwise, are required VCF Policies and Procedures
      • Official personnel roster. VCF Policies and Procedures
      • Police memo book submitted with the front cover including supervisory signature and opening and closing dates VCF Policies and Procedures
    • Residence documents (corroborating)
      • The documents listed below are not sufficient on their own, but will be reviewed in conjunction with other documents VCF Policies and Procedures
    • School or daycare records
      • If you submit a certified transcript or school record as explained below, you do not need to submit any additional proof of presence VCF Policies and Procedures
      • Alumni of Stuyvesant or any area school in the NYC Exposure Zone may email VCF Policies and Procedures info@stuyhealth.org
    • Witness statements (secondary)
      • Statements as the only proof need a minimum of two people VCF Policies and Procedures
      • At least one must be from an individual who is not related to you or to the Personal Representative or Guardian VCF Policies and Procedures
      • Effective February 1, 2023, the VCF will no longer accept affidavits as proof of presence. VCF Policies and Procedures

    Official pages: Eligibility criteria and deadlines (Policies and Procedures, section 1) · How to prove presence (Interactive Presence Guide) · NYC Exposure Zone map

    Turn a question into an MCP query

    MCP routes

    Did the City catalog change after this toolkit’s saved capture?

    portal_changes_since → portal_get_document

    Use two accepted local captures. The result can report observed additions, absences, or changed metadata; it does not prove why a record changed or that a City release is complete.

    Boundary: This checkout’s published capture is dated 2026-09-11. A current comparison needs a newer accepted capture.

    What does a cited City page actually say?

    catalog_search → portal_get_document → portal_get_page_text → citations_verify

    Find the local catalog entry, open its record, read the specific Bates page, then verify the exact quotation. Page text may be withheld when the PII screen flags it.

    Boundary: A located phrase is evidence location, not a finding about exposure, health, liability, or program eligibility.

    Why might an independent project show more search detail?

    portal_catalog_stats → portal_browse

    This MCP reports its own snapshot, catalog structure, and evidence warnings. Another project can have a later capture or different OCR. Compare source PDF, Bates page, capture date, and stated method.

    Boundary: Do not combine counts, map pins, OCR, or tags across projects as if they were one verified dataset.

    Are City portal documents the same as newly declassified federal records?

    citations_verify

    No. This MCP can verify registered City-portal pages and its registered sources; it does not search or interpret the federal release.

    Boundary: Use the National Archives source directly for federal records and identify the record system in every citation.
