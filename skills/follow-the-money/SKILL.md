# Follow the money

## Steps

1. Call `budget_lookup`, with `topic` (portal, doi, education) if the question names one.
2. For each commitment, give the announced amount with its source, then each of the five stages
   with its state: located, not linked, observed, partially observed, not observed, unable to check
   or upcoming.
3. For DOI, call `doi_milestones` and `upcoming_dates` and give the dated reporting obligations
   under Res. 560-A beside the funding rows.
4. Give the commitment's open questions as listed.

## Rules

- Never add amounts together. The commitments come from different announcements and fiscal years.
- An unlinked stage is unknown. Do not describe it as zero, unspent or missing.
- Quote amounts exactly as the source prints them, including "$3.81 million" and "over $3 million"
  where both appear.
